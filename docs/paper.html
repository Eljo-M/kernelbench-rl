Abstract

This paper documents two selected positive examples of GPU kernel generation on an 8 GB laptop: fused bias plus GELU and matrix multiplication plus bias plus SiLU. Both saved programs were generated by a Qwen2.5-Coder-7B-Instruct adapter checkpoint and passed the recorded FP16 verification procedure. The paper explains each computation, the generated dataflow, correctness checks, and CUDA-graph timing boundaries. The result is 2/2 verified featured programs, selected after verification; it is not a complete-evaluation accuracy estimate. These case studies make no claim of incremental reinforcement-learning benefit or application-level acceleration.

The two featured programs

  • Bias + GELUY = GELU_tanh(X + bias)

  • Matrix multiply + bias + SiLUY = SiLU(A @ B + bias)
